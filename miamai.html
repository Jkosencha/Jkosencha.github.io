The Brief

Mia Mai supplies food, hospitality products, packaging and uniforms to businesses and institutions. They needed a professional site that explains what they do, shows their product range and turns visitors into quote requests.

What I Built

  • Clear first impression: a hero that says what the company does in one line, with "Get in Touch" and "Get Quote" calls to action.
  • Product catalogue covering seafood, poultry, beef, fresh farm produce, dry foods, packaging, staff uniforms and wines.
  • Credibility sections: vision, mission, years in business and client numbers, with animated counters.
  • Responsive and lightweight: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework, so it loads quickly on mobile data.
